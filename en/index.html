Siyao Zheng

Assistant Professor, School of International and Public Affairs, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

My research focuses on artificial intelligence for social science research, digital politics, and state governance. I am currently based at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Tsinghua University and was a visiting scholar at Harvard University's Department of Government.
